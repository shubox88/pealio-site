Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Pealio is an AI-powered brand, persona, content, and social publishing software product operated by Paper Wrench ("Pealio", "Paper Wrench", "we", "our", or "us").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use Pealio, including its AI persona, content-generation, campaign, publishing, analytics, website, lead-capture, and connected-service features.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Pealio, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information, including your name, email address, workspace details, and authentication information.
  • Brand information, such as brand names, descriptions, guidelines, tone of voice, positioning, products, services, visual assets, logos, and other materials you provide.
  • Persona and influencer configuration, including character descriptions, communication preferences, appearance references, campaign roles, and other settings used to maintain persona consistency.
  • Knowledge and source materials uploaded or connected by users, including documents, text, campaign briefs, websites, business information, and other reference material.
  • Content and campaign information, including campaign objectives, plans, drafts, posts, images, videos, captions, schedules, approvals, publishing records, and performance information.
  • Social account information made available through services that you authorize Pealio to connect to.
  • Lead or customer information submitted through supported website widgets, forms, campaign interactions, or connected business systems where enabled.
  • Usage and technical information, such as logs, browser and device information, IP address, error reports, security events, and product usage information.

2. AI-Generated Content

Pealio may use artificial intelligence systems to generate or assist with text, images, video concepts, campaign plans, recommendations, persona outputs, summaries, and other content.

Inputs provided to these features may be processed by third-party AI service providers where necessary to deliver the requested functionality.

Pealio does not intentionally submit information to an AI provider unless that information is required for the relevant feature or workflow.

3. LinkedIn Integration

If you choose to connect LinkedIn to Pealio, Pealio may receive information made available through LinkedIn's authorized APIs and the permissions that you approve.

Depending on the permissions granted and the Pealio features you use, this may include:

  • Your LinkedIn member identifier.
  • Your name and profile information where permitted.
  • Your email address where permitted.
  • Information required to publish or manage content through authorized LinkedIn APIs.
  • Organization or Company Page information where the user has appropriate permissions and LinkedIn permits access.
  • Content, publishing receipts, campaign information, engagement data, or analytics made available through approved LinkedIn APIs.
  • Authentication tokens required to maintain the authorized LinkedIn connection.

Pealio does not obtain or store your LinkedIn password.

LinkedIn actions are performed only through authorized APIs and subject to the permissions granted by the user and the capabilities approved by LinkedIn.

Where Pealio supports content approval, users may be required to review and approve content before publication.

4. Other Social and Third-Party Integrations

Pealio may support connections with other social networks, content platforms, business applications, CRM systems, analytics providers, AI services, authentication providers, hosting providers, and other third-party services.

The information available to Pealio depends on the service, the permissions you grant, and the features that are enabled.

Use of third-party services remains subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate Pealio.
  • Authenticate users and manage workspaces.
  • Create and maintain AI personas and brand representations.
  • Ground generated content in user-provided brand and knowledge information.
  • Create campaign strategies, plans, drafts, images, and other content.
  • Support approval, scheduling, and authorized publishing workflows.
  • Measure content and campaign performance.
  • Generate analytics, learning insights, and optimization recommendations.
  • Maintain connected social and business integrations.
  • Process leads and campaign interactions where those features are used.
  • Provide support and respond to requests.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, security threats, and technical failures.
  • Improve product reliability, usability, and functionality.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

6. Human Approval and Automated Actions

Certain Pealio workflows may support content generation, recommendations, scheduling, or automated execution.

Where a workflow requires user approval, Pealio will not intentionally publish or execute the applicable action until the required approval has been provided.

If a user enables more automated functionality in the future, the user remains responsible for configuring appropriate permissions, approvals, guardrails, and connected accounts.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Pealio, including providers of:

  • Cloud infrastructure and hosting.
  • Authentication and account management.
  • Database and storage services.
  • Artificial intelligence services.
  • Email and notification services.
  • Monitoring, security, and error reporting.
  • Social and business integrations selected by users.
  • Billing and payment processing where applicable.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to Pealio, subject to their applicable agreements and policies.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, Paper Wrench, or the security of the service.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and security records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

Social authentication tokens and integration credentials are retained only for as long as needed to maintain connections authorized by the user or as otherwise required for security and compliance purposes.

9. Social Account Disconnection

Users may disconnect supported social integrations from Pealio when the relevant feature is available.

Users may also revoke application access directly through the applicable third-party platform, such as LinkedIn.

After access is revoked, Pealio will no longer be able to use the revoked authorization for future API actions.
